# WorkEasy 🛠️

WorkEasy is an open, cooperative-first on-demand skilled labor platform designed to connect verified service professionals with customers with 0% platform commission, upfront fixed fare computation, and quality assurance.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Ride-Hailing Style Upfront Fixed Pricing**: Transparent fare calculation based on skill tier, distance, and transit allowance.
- **Material Procurement Options**: Choose between providing materials yourself or having the partner procure standardized replacement parts.
- **Bulk Workforce Dispatch**:
  - **Option 1: Licensed Contractor Squad**: Certified crew leads with on-site supervision and heavy commercial equipment.
  - **Option 2: Cooperative Skill Pooling**: Algorithmic synchronization of multiple independent certified workers into a unified dispatch unit.
- **Tyre Puncture & Roadside Assistance**: Dedicated 8–15 minute express dispatch for 2-wheelers and 4-wheelers.
- **2 to 4 Days Quality Guarantee**: Three resolution paths (free same-partner revisit, subsidized replacement partner, or escrow settlement).
- **Skill Apprenticeship & Mentorship**: Low-level workers shadowing master craftsmen for certified tier upgrades.
- **Tipping & Direct Escrow Release**: Direct-to-worker gratuity with 0% intermediary deduction.

## 🚀 Getting Started

\`\`\`

\`\`\`bash

\`\`\`
